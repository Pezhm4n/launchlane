# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome different levels of experience,
explain technical disagreements with evidence, and respect other people's work.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and disclosure of private information are
not acceptable in issues, reviews, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive material, close disruptive conversations,
and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors. Decisions should
be proportionate and explain the conduct involved without revealing private details.

For sensitive conduct reports, ask a maintainer for a private channel without posting
personal information. A dedicated confidential contact has not yet been published.
